02 — Investment Principle · 投資原則
尺度不變性
Scale Invariance
不論初始本金多少，以相同的利率運行相同的時間，複利成長的倍數永遠相同。資本的規模不改變法則的本質，每一個投資組合——無論大小——都接受同一套原則的檢驗。
Regardless of initial principal, equal rates over equal time yield the same growth multiple. The scale of capital does not alter the principle. Every portfolio — small or large — compounds by the same mathematical law.
03 — Compound · 複利
時間，是最可靠的錨點
Time: The Most Reliable Anchor
在波動的金融世界裡，複利提供的不是最快的回報，而是最可計算的成長軌跡。

對長期投資者而言，財富累積的核心命題從來不是「今年賺了多少」，而是「紀律是否足夠——是否讓時間發揮了它應有的複利效應」。
In volatile markets, compounding delivers not the fastest returns, but the most calculable growth trajectory. Wealth accumulation is not about how much you earned this year. It is about whether discipline was sufficient — whether time was allowed to do what only time can do.
